# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Automotive, industrial, and embedded systems engineers, and hobbyists debugging J1939 or CANopen hardware. They need to view high-density CAN frame logs, map database signals, and simulate nodes in real-time under high cognitive loads.

## Product Purpose
Provide a beautiful, high-performance desktop CAN bus analyzer and simulation tool as a modern, typography-centric alternative to Kvaser CanKing.

## Brand Personality
Teenage Engineering aesthetic: quiet, precise, industrial, typographic whispers. Thin text, sharp cuts, extreme restraint.
- 3 words: Machined, Quiet, Pure.

## Anti-references
- SaaS dashboards with soft shadows, purple gradients, and rounded card containers.
- Crowded Windows-95 style industrial control panels.
- Saturated, decorative colors that carry no semantic meaning.

## Design Principles
1. **The Data is the Hero**: Visual emphasis is placed on raw packet logs and signal waveforms, framed like physical products in a catalogue under studio lights.
2. **Hairline Structure**: Sections are divided by 1px hairlines (`#e5e5e5` or `#000000`) instead of background colors or drop shadows.
3. **Typographic Restraint**: Text sits in ultra-thin 100/300 weight Inter/Suisse sans-serif, using size and spacing rather than weight or color for hierarchy.
4. **Rationed Accent**: Color is strictly restricted to neutrals, saving chromatic accents (like warning amber or red) for active state and error alerts.
5. **Machined Edges**: Absolute 0px border radius on all buttons, panels, tables, and inputs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Contrast ratio ≥ 4.5:1 for body copy against the light Canvas Mist (`#f6f8f7`) or dark Carbon (`#0f0e12`) background.
- Support for reduced motion (instant transitions instead of animations).
